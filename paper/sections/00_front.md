# FlowLens: Explainable Real-Time Network Anomaly Detection with TreeSHAP and Two-Tier Monitoring

Vedika · Krishita Gupta · Krish Singh
School of Computer Science and Engineering, Vellore Institute of Technology

## Abstract

Traffic monitoring tools typically separate traffic visibility from security alerts. When a machine-learning detector raises an alert, the analyst receives a label without reasoning. Explainable intrusion detection work almost always attaches SHAP explanations to alerts, but offline and usually with Kernel SHAP, a model-agnostic estimating technique, that is far too slow to perform per connection on live traffic. We claim that per-flow explanations are cheap enough for real-time monitoring with the combination of two designs: tree-based detectors explained with TreeSHAP, which computes exact Shapley values in polynomial time, and a two-tier architecture that runs full scoring and explanation only when abnormal traffic is observed. We implement this design as FlowLens, which replays labelled CIC-IDS2017 flows as a one-second-window stream. An EWMA monitor switches between NORMAL and ELEVATED states. LightGBM classifies known attack types, an Isolation Forest trained only on benign traffic detects unseen ones, and TreeSHAP presents the top contributing features in an understandable form on a live dashboard. On the test split, LightGBM reaches a macro F1 of [lgbm_macro_f1] with a benign false-positive rate of [lgbm_benign_fpr]. TreeSHAP explains a flow in [tree_p50] ms at the median, against [kernel_p50] ms for Kernel SHAP, a [shap_speedup]× speed-up. With at most 300 explanations per window, end-to-end processing takes [p95_e2e_ms] ms per one-second window at the 95th percentile under stress load. Unseen attacks remain the weak point: with [holdout_class] withheld from training, LightGBM alone caught [holdout_lgbm_recall] of its flows, the Isolation Forest [holdout_iforest_recall] and the two together [holdout_either_recall], and with Bot withheld neither detector caught any. Low-and-slow attacks that do not change traffic volume are caught only at the sampling rate.

**Keywords:** network anomaly detection, explainable AI, SHAP, TreeSHAP, LightGBM, Isolation Forest, real-time monitoring, CIC-IDS2017
