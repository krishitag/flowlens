Limitations

What tiering trades away, and what we can't claim

Low-and-slow attacks

A slow port scan barely changes volume. In slowscan.json the monitor stayed NORMAL for the whole run, so the scan was caught only by the 10% sample.

Dataset artefacts

CIC-IDS2017 is a testbed capture with known labelling issues. We exclude ports, IPs and timestamps from features, but real networks may score lower.

Replayed, not captured

Latency excludes packet capture and flow-feature extraction, which a deployment would add.

Not user-tested

We measure how fast explanations are, not how much they help analysts. That needs a user study.
